# Two Hearts Beat (@twoheartsbeat.live) Instagram Automation Engine

Autonomous cloud publishing and community management system for [Two Hearts Beat](https://twoheartsbeat.vercel.app/).

---

## 📱 How to Use From Your Phone (No PC / Laptop Needed)

### Option A: The GitHub Mobile App (Zero PC Dependency)
1. Download the **GitHub Mobile app** on your iPhone ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/github/id1477376905)) or Android ([Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.github.android)).
2. Log in with your account (`soumyadeepsarkar-2004`).
3. Open this repository: **`twoheartsbeat-automation`**.
4. Tap **Actions** ➔ **Daily 7:30 PM Instagram Publisher**.
5. Tap **Run workflow** anytime to publish immediately from your phone, OR let the automated schedule trigger automatically at **7:30 PM IST sharp** every day!
6. You will receive push notifications on your phone whenever a post is published.

---

### Option B: Mobile Web Command Center (PWA)
1. Open the mobile dashboard link on Safari (iOS) or Chrome (Android).
2. Tap **Share** ➔ **Add to Home Screen** to install it as an app icon on your phone!
3. One-tap buttons:
   - 🚀 **Publish to Feed Now**
   - 💬 **Sweep Comments & DM "SYNC" Links**
   - 📊 **Check Real-Time Insights & Views**
   - 🎬 **Watch 9:16 Video Previews**

---

## ⏰ Daily Automation Schedule
- **Schedule:** Every day at **19:30 IST (7:30 PM)**.
- **Redundancy:** Cloud (GitHub Actions) + Local (Windows Task Scheduler `TwoHeartsBeatDailyAutomation`).
- **Features:** Automated Reel/Carousel publishing, live comment monitoring, and instant `SYNC` link delivery.
